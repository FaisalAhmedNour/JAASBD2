Certification Type

Two questions decide which certificate you need

The product names (Positive, Premium, EV) hide a simple choice. It comes down to how much identity checking you want, and how many names the padlock has to cover.

DV ┬╖ Domain Validation

Proves you control the domain and issues within minutes. The entry level, and enough for a straightforward brochure or blog site.

EV ┬╖ Extended Validation

The strictest identity checks. Your verified legal name is recorded in the certificate. For banks, large stores, and high-trust brands.

Wildcard

One domain plus every subdomain under it (shop., blog., app.). A single certificate covers unlimited subdomains at once.

OV ┬╖ Organisation Validation

We verify your organisation is real, and your business details sit inside the certificate. For businesses that handle customer data.

Single domain

One site, one certificate. The right choice when your whole site lives on a single address with no separate subdomains.

Multi-domain

Several separate domains secured on one certificate. Useful when you run more than one brand or address from a single place.

How it works

What happens after you order

Start by choosing the level of validation you need. Not sure which? Tell us about your site and we will point you to the right certificate.

Then we verify your details. That takes a few minutes for a domain-validated certificate, and up to a couple of days for organisation or extended validation. Once you are verified, we issue the certificate, install it on your server, and switch your site to HTTPS. We test that every page loads securely before we call it done.

After setup we stay on hand. If a certificate needs renewing or reissuing, or anything looks off, support is available 24/7/365.
