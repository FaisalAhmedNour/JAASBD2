Why go bare metal?

A cloud server gives you a virtual slice of shared hardware. It is fast to deploy and easy to resize, and for most workloads it is the right call. A dedicated server gives you the physical machine itself. Here is when that matters.

Consistent performance under load

No hypervisor between you and the hardware, and no other tenants on the box. The performance you benchmark is the performance you keep, every core and every IOP.

More storage than cloud servers reach

900GB of usable NVMe as standard on every configuration, with larger disk options available. If your databases or media libraries have outgrown cloud server tiers, this is where they go.

Your network, not a shared one

A dedicated 1 Gbps connection belongs to the machine, with 10 Gbps available. Heavy transfer workloads do not queue behind anyone else.
